Notus is a classic, elegant and light serif family engineered by Wiktor Buksza. It fuses smooth, flowing serif endings (its signature visual motif) with high-contrast contemporary architecture, delivering monumental drama in headlines and crystalline legibility in extended text settings.

The collection is developed across three distinct optical sizes: Display for dramatic titles, Text for prolonged long-form reading, and Caption for dense footnotes, tables, and packaging specifications.

Designed with native linguistic sensitivity. Complex Cyrillic characters like Ж, б, and я are crafted from foundational Slavic stroke logic rather than forced Latin adaptations, preserving cultural resonance and fluid typographic balance.

Supporting small caps, proportional old-style numerals, tabular figures, discretionary ligatures, and arbitrary fractions. Available in OTF, TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 formats.
