Optical Hierarchy

Three engineered masters tailored for optical physics: Display for impact, Text for continuous reading, Caption for micro-clarity.

Optical Size 01

Notus Display

Optimized for 42pt and above

Elegance
Extreme stroke contrast, hairline serifs, sculpted terminals, and narrow proportions crafted for dramatic titles, poster headlines, and editorial covers.
Optical Size 02

Notus Text

Optimized for 12pt to 24pt

Balanced rhythm, robust bracketed serifs, and proportional counters for effortless long-form reading.
Tempered contrast and durable joins ensure the font holds strong ink-traps and maintains steady black-and-white balance in paragraphs.
Optical Size 03

Notus Caption

Optimized for 6pt to 10pt

Engineered for footnotes, data tables, packaging specifications, and legal micro-typography. Open apertures remain crisp even in dense environments.
Generous x-height, widened apertures, sturdier hairlines, and loose natural tracking prevent clogging at minimal physical scales.
03

Signature Anatomy

A closer inspection of the details that define Notus: flowing terminals, high-contrast modulation, and native Cyrillic drawing.

Qg&

Flowing Serif Endings

The hallmark of Notus: graceful, fluid curved terminations that break the rigid conventions of neoclassical serifs, imbuing every letterform with kinetic warmth and organic elegance.

Жбя

Authentic Cyrillic Architecture

Designed with native linguistic sensitivity. Complex Cyrillic characters like Ж, б, and я are crafted from foundational stroke logic rather than forced Latin adaptations, preserving cultural resonance.

Vex

Sculpted Fast Italics

The italics feature an agile, expressive slant with calligraphic entrance and exit terminals. They offer immediate visual contrast against the upright styles in editorial composition.

019§

Old-style Figures & Symbols

Includes proportional old-style numerals tailored for body text, lining figures for headlines, tabular numerals for financial tables, and an extensive system of editorial symbols.
